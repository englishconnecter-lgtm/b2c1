B2 Grammar · Reported Speech

Use reported speech to summarise what people said, asked or told someone to do.

  • Statements: Use said (that) or told + person + (that). After a past reporting verb, the tense often shifts back: are → were, showed → had shown, can → could and will → would. Backshift can be optional if the information is still true.
  • Questions: Use asked if/whether for yes/no questions. Keep the question word in wh-questions, and use statement word order: He asked how the study worked.
  • Requests and instructions: Use asked/told + person + (not) to + verb.
  • Pronouns and time words: Change them if the speaker or time has changed, for example “I’ll call you tomorrow” → She said she would call me the following day.

Report what was said

Choose the best reported version. Use backshift where appropriate. Some examples are adapted for practice.

2. “We showed participants four photos.”

Thought-Provoking Questions

  2. Can social media change the way we remember our lives?
  3. Why might smells be stronger memory triggers than photos?
  4. Should people worry about false memories?
  5. How could memory cues help elderly people or people with dementia?
  6. Would you want to perfectly record every moment of your life?
