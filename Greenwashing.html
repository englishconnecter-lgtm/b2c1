The video reports increased sales of goods marketed as sustainable. What does that figure establish, and what would you need to show that the claims drove the increase?

It establishes sales growth for that category. To isolate the effect of sustainability claims, you would need a comparison with similar products and evidence accounting for other influences such as price, availability and promotion.

A company cites offsets and a distant emissions target. Why might the two together sound more convincing than the evidence warrants?

Offsets may depend on reductions that would have happened anyway. A distant target also leaves current progress unclear. Near-term milestones and separately reported figures for direct cuts and offsets would help readers assess the claim.

C1 Grammar · Gerunds and Infinitives

  • Change of meaning. Stop using means discontinue use. Stop to use means interrupt another action in order to use something.
  • Regret. Regret having made a claim expresses regret about a past action. Regret to inform you introduces unwelcome news formally.
  • Consequences and intentions. Meeting the target means redesigning the process. Here, means introduces a consequence. In We meant to redesign the process, it expresses an intention.
  • Earlier actions and passives. Having disclosed and to have disclosed can mark an earlier action. In the passive, use forms such as having been audited or to have been audited.
